# Returns FAQ (customer friendly)

**How long do I have to return something?**
You have 30 days from the day your order is delivered.

**How will I get my money back?**
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.

**Can I get a refund for an order that has not arrived?**
Not yet. Please track your order; if it never arrives, contact us again after the expected delivery date.

**Can I get more than one refund for the same order?**
No. Each order can be refunded once.

**Who approves bigger refunds?**
Larger refunds are reviewed by our support team, and the biggest ones also by finance. This keeps your money safe.
