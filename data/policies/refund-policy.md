# TechNova Retail Refund Policy

Version 3. Owner: Customer Operations policy team. Rules are numbered so that
tickets, tools and audits can quote them.

## 1.1 Delivered orders only
Only orders with status "delivered" can be refunded. An order that is placed,
shipped or cancelled is not refundable; the customer should track the order instead.

## 1.2 Orders still on the way
If an order has not arrived yet, we share the tracking status and the expected delivery date. We do not refund it.

## 2.1 Thirty day window
A refund must be requested within 30 days of the delivery date.

## 2.2 Day counting
Day 30 after delivery is still accepted. From day 31 onward the request is late and is declined.

## 3.1 Small refunds
A refund of Rs 2,000 or less is approved automatically when every other rule is met.

## 3.2 Medium refunds
A refund from Rs 2,001 up to Rs 25,000 needs approval from one support lead.

## 3.3 Large refunds
A refund above Rs 25,000 needs approval from a support lead AND from finance. These must be two different people.

## 3.4 Amount limits
A refund can never be more than the order price, and it can never be zero or negative.

## 4.1 One refund per order
An order can be refunded at most once. If a refund already exists we tell the customer its id and status.

## 4.2 Repeated requests
Repeating the same request must never pay twice. The same request key always returns the same refund.

## 5.1 Additional review
Some accounts need extra review. For these accounts a support lead must approve a refund at any amount.

## 6.1 Active platform incidents
When a ticket is linked to an open platform incident, no individual refund is issued. The customer receives an incident update instead.
